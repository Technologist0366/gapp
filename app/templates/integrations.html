🚀 Available Integrations

Google Workspace

Connect your own Google account to pull files, Docs, Sheets (including Google Forms responses), and folders as evidence. Sync to controls/projects.

Microsoft 365

Connect your own Microsoft account to pull files from OneDrive/SharePoint, Outlook emails, and Teams as evidence.

Email (Gmail / Outlook)

Connect your email to collect audit emails, approvals, notifications, and evidence correspondence.

Dropbox

Connect your Dropbox to pull shared folders, documents, and evidence files.

Jira

Connect your Jira account to link tickets/issues as evidence.

Slack / Microsoft Teams

Connect your Slack or Teams to get alerts and share evidence links.

WhatsApp Business

Connect your WhatsApp Business account to send/receive alerts and evidence requests.
