About ISO 22000:2018

The International Organization for Standardization (ISO) developed ISO 22000:2018, titled Food Safety Management Systems — Requirements for Any Organization in the Food Chain. This standard provides a framework to ensure food safety across the entire food supply chain, from primary production to consumption. It is designed to help organizations consistently deliver safe products and services that meet customer, statutory, and regulatory requirements. The framework is structured around ten clauses, covering key areas such as:

  • Context of the organization
  • Hazard analysis and control
  • Prerequisite programmes (PRPs)
  • Traceability systems
  • Emergency preparedness

ISO 22000:2018 integrates the Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) cycle and Codex HACCP principles to identify, assess, and control food safety hazards. It emphasizes risk-based thinking at both organizational and operational levels, ensuring that organizations can prevent or reduce food safety risks effectively. By implementing this standard, organizations can enhance food safety, improve customer trust, and achieve compliance with global regulations.

In conclusion, ISO 22000:2018 is a vital framework for organizations in the food chain, providing comprehensive guidance to ensure the safety of food products. By adhering to its requirements, organizations can mitigate risks, maintain compliance, and safeguard consumer health, making it an essential standard for food safety management.
