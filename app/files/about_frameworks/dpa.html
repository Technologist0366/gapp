About the Data Protection Act (Kenya)

The Data Protection Act (DPA), enacted in 2019, is Kenya’s primary law governing privacy and data protection. It establishes principles and obligations for the processing of personal data to safeguard the privacy of individuals while enabling responsible use of data in digital and physical systems.

The DPA creates the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC), which is mandated to regulate, enforce, and provide guidance on data protection practices. Organizations that handle personal data are required to adopt governance, technical, and organizational controls to ensure lawful and secure processing. Key control areas under the DPA include:

  • Lawful basis for processing personal data (consent, contract, legal obligation, legitimate interest, etc.)
  • Transparency through clear and accessible privacy notices
  • Data minimization — collecting only data necessary for the stated purpose
  • Accuracy and timely correction of personal data
  • Storage limitation — retaining data only for as long as necessary
  • Integrity and confidentiality — protecting data through technical and organizational security measures
  • Registration of data controllers and processors with the ODPC
  • Rights of data subjects including access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction, and portability
  • Special protections for sensitive personal data (e.g., health, biometric, and children’s data)
  • Cross-border data transfer restrictions and adequacy requirements
  • Mandatory breach notification to the ODPC and affected data subjects
  • Privacy by design and default in systems and services
  • Conducting Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) for high-risk processing

The DPA empowers the ODPC to conduct investigations, issue enforcement and compliance notices, impose administrative fines, and suspend data processing operations. Organizations are expected to maintain policies, train employees, and implement safeguards to demonstrate accountability and compliance.

By adhering to the DPA, organizations can build trust, ensure legal compliance, and enhance responsible digital transformation in Kenya.
